Panzerchess

Panzerchess is a strategy game of tank warfare on a grid-based board. The goal of the game is to capture the enemy base or destroy all enemy units. Each player has a tank battalion that consists of: two Light Tanks, one Medium Tank, one Heavy Tank, one Tank Destroyer and one Artillery.

Play Panzerchess online

Cubolino

Cubolino is a tactical game for two players. The goal of the game is to finish with most of the cubes of your colour facing up. Each player starts with eight Cubes and the game is played on a 4x4 board.

Play Cubolino online

Battle Stations!

Battle Stations! is a game of naval combat for two players. The goal of the game is to destroy all enemy ships or win on points after a stalemate. Each player has a fleet of ships that consists of: two Submarines, two Destroyers, one Missile Destroyer, two Light Cruisers, two Battlecruisers, one Battleship and two Frigates.
